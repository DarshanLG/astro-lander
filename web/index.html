What the project found

  • PPO written from scratch scores 273 on 100 held-out landings, against 276 for Stable-Baselines3 with the same settings.
  • Textbook reward shaping crashed every time. Ending an episode paid out the potential, and impact made the lander look better. Leaving the final step unshaped fixed it: 100% on the pad.
  • Training in random weather cut crashes in wind from 36% to 11% in Python stress tests, with no cost in calm air.
